# Norma — Brand Identity Brief

> **Reconciled 2026-07-17 to the RUNWAY ethos.** Norma is **not a budget, a tracker, or a journal.**
> It answers one question: **how long does your money last?** The earlier "financial journal / write
> it down" framing (notes, voice capture, receipt capture, "what you keep meaning to buy") is
> **retired** — those features were cut or made dormant in the July runway-first pivot. Audience is a
> **psychographic — "The Escape-Minded" — not a demographic** (nomads are the beachhead, not the
> ceiling). Color tokens still live in `mauldin/norma-landing-page-brand-spec.md`, but the in-app
> palette below (dark + lavender) is the source of truth for the app itself.

## What Norma is
Norma turns your money into **time.** You tell it your balance and your bills; it shows your
**runway** — how many days of freedom you have — and keeps that number honest as life moves. No
budgets, no categories, no dashboards, no receipts to file. One glance tells you whether you're
alright. When the runway grows, that's the whole point — and Norma makes it feel like it.

## Who it's for — "The Escape-Minded"
People who measure wealth in **freedom, not totals** — united by wanting *out* (or staying out): the
pursuit of "fuck you money." The line is **wants freedom vs. content on the treadmill,** not
"self-funded vs. salaried." A salaried person plotting their exit is as much the user as a nomad
already living off savings.

**Two postures, one mind:**
- **Building** — has income, wants out. Watches runway **grow** toward escape velocity.
- **Living** — off a finite pool. Watches runway **hold**, stays free longer.

Escape isn't a one-time event: builders are *planning* it, the already-free are *defending* it.

**What they share (the psychographic):**
- Want **freedom over a higher score.**
- **Reject dashboards and budgets** — they won't maintain a system.
- Want an **at-a-glance "am I alright"** — reassurance, not analysis.
- Feel real **satisfaction when the runway moves the right way.**
- Often **cash-heavy / multi-currency,** so bank-sync can't see them — which is *why* manual balance
  re-anchoring is the right architecture, not a compromise.

**Beachhead → beyond nomads:** freelancers between contracts, sabbatical-takers, coast/lean-FIRE,
bootstrapped founders paying themselves from savings, lumpy-income creatives, and salaried people
plotting an exit.

**NOT our user:** salaried optimizers who love a dashboard, or anyone who *wants* a budgeting system.
Runway means little to someone whose account refills, untouched, on the 1st.

## The promise
**"Know how many days your money lasts — without a budget."**

## Core brand attributes
- **Freeing** — money reframed as time and autonomy, not obligation.
- **Honest** — one number, re-anchored by you; never faked, never a guilt trip.
- **Calm** — reassurance at a glance; anxiety-reducing, never a doom clock.
- **Effortless** — no categories, no maintenance, no dashboards.
- **Aspirational** — a growing runway is a flex; we celebrate progress toward freedom.

## Voice & tone
- Talks about **freedom and time,** not spending and discipline.
- **Celebrates the runway getting longer;** never shames a short one.
- Plain, warm, a little **irreverent** (the FU-money wink) — never corporate-finance.
- Uses the native vocabulary: *runway, days, free, anchor, burn, escape velocity* — never *budget,
  category, expense report, savings goal.*

## Visual Design System

> The pastel-teal / conversational-bubble system described in earlier versions was the **old journal
> UI** and is retired. Below is the **current app** (dark + lavender, runway-first).

### Palette — dark + lavender
- **Canvas:** near-black `#16161A` — the app is dark-first.
- **Accent:** lavender (`NormaPalette.lavender`, user-selectable accent theme) — the single
  interactive / emphasis color.
- **Text:** cream `#F2EDE2` primary; muted + dim grays for hierarchy.
- **Semantic:** soft green = "runway live," warm amber = "stale / warning." Desaturated so a low
  runway *informs* without alarming.

### Typography
- **Rubik** — rounded, friendly, legible for numbers.
- **Wave-text** for the runway hero: words animate in one at a time with soft haptics.

### Material & depth
- **Liquid Glass** capsules — translucent, premium.
- **Capsule pattern:** solid border = active, dashed border = inactive (core design language).
- **Top-lit gradient fills** on hero cards (balance capsule, forecast cards).

### Motion & the "thinking" motif
- **Dither shader** = Norma thinking (chat fetch, what-if crunch) — the one salvaged piece of the
  old notes engine.
- **Spring animations** + **soft haptics** as tactile confirmation.

### Signature moments (protect these)
1. **The runway hero** — wave-text, e.g. "94 days of freedom."
2. **The re-anchor moment** — enter your balance → burn + runway reveal (the core reward loop).
3. **The share card** — runway as a *flex,* not a confession (see the business model / virality).

## Design principles
1. **One number first** — runway is the hero; everything supports it.
2. **Freedom framing** — time, not dollars; growth celebrated.
3. **Glance, don't analyze** — reassurance over dashboards.
4. **Honest by design** — you anchor the truth; the app never fakes it.
5. **Calm authority** — never alarm, never shame.

## Reference aesthetic
A **flight instrument for your freedom** — the calm and polish of a premium iOS app with the wink of
the FU-money mindset. Think altitude / runway gauge, not spreadsheet.
