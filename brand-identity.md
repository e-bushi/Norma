## App Overview
Norma is a **financial journal that gets money out of your head.** You write what's happening in
plain English — by voice or a snapped receipt — and Norma makes sense of what's due, what's coming,
and what you keep meaning to buy. It's for people who tried the budgeting apps (Copilot, Mint,
spreadsheets) and quit them all. Explicitly **not a budget, not a tracker** — a journal that talks
back. The emphasis is approachability and relief: clarity without intimidation.

## Core Brand Attributes
- **Approachable**: Financial management without intimidation
- **Intelligent**: AI-powered insights presented conversationally
- **Calm**: Reduces financial anxiety through clear, organized information
- **Modern**: Contemporary design leveraging latest iOS capabilities
- **Personal**: One-on-one conversation with a helpful assistant

## Visual Design System

### Color Palette
**Muted Pastels** - Soft, calming tones that reduce financial stress, desaturated so financial data
feels soothing rather than alarming. Current tokens + usage live in `norma-landing-page-brand-spec.md`
(dark-mode background `#231c18`, pastel-teal as primary CTA). Roles, **not budget categories**:
- Pastel Teal: primary CTA, interactive elements, links
- Pastel Purple: secondary accent, badges
- Pastel Coral: emphasis / attention
- Neutral warm background: clean, uncluttered canvas
- Dark accents: grounding elements

**Philosophy**: Colors are desaturated to feel soothing rather than alarming, even when showing financial data that might typically cause stress.

### Typography
**Rounded, friendly, yet professional** - The type system should feel:
- Approachable (rounded terminals, open apertures)
- Legible (critical for financial data/numbers)
- Contemporary (modern fintech aesthetic)
- Hierarchical (clear distinction between chat messages, data, and labels)

### UI Components

**Buttons & Interactive Elements**:
- Capsule shapes with generous padding
- Soft, filled backgrounds (no harsh borders)
- Colored tags for categories (pills/badges)
- Circular send button (dark, confident)

**Cards & Containers**:
- Rounded rectangles (28pt corner radius)
- Subtle borders (very low opacity)
- Soft shadows (minimal, implies floating)
- Liquid Glass effect (iOS 26) - translucent, reflective backgrounds that feel premium and modern

**Chat Interface**:
- Conversational bubbles with asymmetric design
- User messages: subtle tint
- AI responses: incorporate data visualizations inline
- Typing indicators with animation
- Generous spacing for breathing room

### Material & Depth
- **Liquid Glass**: Primary material for input areas - suggests intelligence, transparency, premium quality
- **Layering**: Subtle depth through shadows and translucency
- **Gradients**: Minimal, used for fade effects rather than decoration
- **Elevation**: Floating elements feel light and accessible

### Motion & Animation
- **Spring animations** (response: 0.4, damping: 0.8) - playful yet controlled
- **Transitions**: Scale + opacity combinations for content appearing
- **Haptic feedback**: Tactile confirmation of important actions
- **Auto-scroll**: Smooth, guided focus on conversation flow

### Mood & Tone
**Visual Mood**: Calm confidence - like talking to a knowledgeable friend over coffee
**Emotional Goal**: Transform financial anxiety into financial empowerment
**Spatial Feel**: Airy, uncluttered, focused - not overwhelming with data

### Design Principles
1. **Conversation First**: The chat is the primary interface; everything supports dialogue
2. **Data in Context**: Numbers and charts appear as part of natural conversation, not separate dashboards
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: Complexity revealed only when needed
4. **Soft Authority**: Professional guidance without corporate coldness
5. **Adaptive Transparency**: Glass effects let content shine through, creating visual depth

### Reference Aesthetic
Think: Modern banking apps meet messaging interfaces, with the premium feel of iOS system apps and the approachability of a personal assistant.
