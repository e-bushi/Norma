## Description (≤4,000 chars)

> Know how many days your money lasts — without a budget.
>
> Your bank shows a balance. Norma turns it into runway: how many days of freedom you have, and
> whether that number is growing. No budgets, no categories, no dashboards to maintain. One glance
> tells you if you're okay.
>
> HOW IT WORKS
> • Tell Norma your balance and your bills.
> • See your runway — your money, measured in days of freedom.
> • Update your balance when it changes, and watch the runway move.
>
> That's it. Norma does the math; you just check in.
>
> BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO WANT OUT
> Freelancers between contracts. Nomads living off savings. Anyone on a sabbatical, building toward
> "enough," or quietly planning their escape. If your money question is "how long can I keep doing
> this?" instead of "am I on budget?", Norma is for you.
>
> WHAT YOU GET
> • Runway — your balance as days of freedom, kept honest as life moves.
> • Bills & paydays — add what's coming; Norma reads it as your burn and your refill.
> • Forecast — see what's left after bills, and a safe-to-spend number.
> • Test a purchase — feel a spend before you make it: "this trip costs you 9 days."
> • Calendar — your low point before payday, and the days money lands.
> • Flexible income — weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or irregular. It fits how you're actually paid.
> • Private by design — your money stays yours.
>
> NOT A BUDGET
> Norma isn't Mint, YNAB, or a spreadsheet. It won't make you categorize a coffee or maintain a
> system. It answers one question — how long does your money last — and gets out of your way.
>
> Free to start. Norma Pro is $3.99/month.
